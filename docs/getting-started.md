1. Under **Format**, enter the width, height and refresh rate, and choose the **Processor** you're sending it to/from.
2. **Results** shows whether it fits one input, with its pixel clock. **Timing** has the EDID timing, **I/O** which input/outputs support it, and **Mac** covers if your Mac supports the format.
3. Download the EDID file and load it into the sender/receiver: BetterDisplay on a Mac, Custom Resolution Utility on Windows, or an EDID emulator.

Format settings can be saved and restored.
